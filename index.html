Service Tickets POD · Problem sizing

A closure made in Kapture never reaches the CSP's card

How big is it, who does it hurt, and is it worth solving? Roadmap item 01, problem 6 — measured against production data, 11 Aug – 10 Sep 2026.

Ashish Raj · PM, Service Tickets POD · 17 Sep 2026 · roadmap
01

Verdict

Build it — but not on the TAT penalty. The gap is real and growing fast: 12.96% of tickets are closed in Kapture before the CSP, and that share went 9.4% → 14.2% in a single month. But removing every unfairly-scored breach moves the platform within-TAT rate by 1.01 points and moves the median affected CSP by zero. The real cost is operational waste — and it is large.

1,078technicians dispatched per month to a fault already closed in Kapture
1,774complaints a month that never close at all — mean age 21 days, clock still running
1,760live, CSP-actionable cards a month for work that no longer exists
+1.01ppall the TAT fairness this fix buys, platform-wide

The study was commissioned on the premise that CSPs are being penalised in Quality for a sync failure. That premise is true, and it is small. What the data found instead is thousands of CSP actions and technician trips spent on jobs that were already finished, and a growing pile of complaints that no system will ever close.

The two shaded leaves are the whole injustice: 535 tickets a month. And the two “after the TAT” branches are why that is not 5,526 — 3,858 tickets (70% of the Kapture-first population) were already late when the agent closed them. Fixing the sync does not save those. That single line is what separates the size of the gap (13.0%) from the size of the injustice (1.26%).

Growing. On matched day 1–10 windows, the Kapture-first share of resolved tickets went 9.43% (August) → 14.24% (September). It cannot be measured before mid-July 2026 — the .NET→Java rewrite of ticket-service is what made the closing actor identifiable at all, so any series starting earlier shows a jump from zero that is instrumentation, not behaviour.

05

The four questions, answered

Q1How many tickets are closed from Kapture first vs the CSP?

12.96% Kapture-first (5,526), 87.0% CSP-first (37,085). See §02. The CSP→Kapture leg is confirmed working — median propagation +0.02 min, P95 +0.03 min — so this is a one-directional gap, exactly as the code says (F-18: SrsClient has only notifyComplaint and notifyReopenComplaint; no close call exists).

Q2Of Kapture-closed tickets, does the CSP mark them — and before or after TAT?

  • 62.7% (3,467/5,526) are eventually marked by the CSP. 37.3% (2,059) never are.
  • Lag from Kapture closure to CSP mark: median 15.2 h · P90 97.7 h (4.1 days).
  • Kapture's own closure was inside TAT on 1,319 of 3,464 (38.1%) class-C tickets; the CSP's mark was inside TAT on 924 (26.7%).
  • When the CSP marks late, they overshoot the deadline by a median 25.1 h.

The number that matters, and it cuts against the premise: 61.9% of class-C tickets were already past TAT when the agent closed them. For those the missing sync did not cause the breach — the CSP was late regardless. Pure sync loss, where Kapture closed inside the deadline and the CSP's mark landed outside it, is 535 tickets a month — 1.26% of the cohort.

Q3How many CSPs are impacted in Quality?

737 / 969 CSPs hit the gap at least once 216 carry ≥1 unfair breach 79 carry ≥2 · 11 carry ≥5 median swing if fixed: 0.00 pp

Verified against the actual scoring input, COMPLAINT_RESOLUTION_LEDGER: of the 395 class-C sync-loss tickets, 393 are scored RESOLVED_WITHIN_TAT = FALSE and none are EXCLUDED_FROM_SCORING. The penalty is real. It is also rare — among the 536 CSPs with ≥20 adjudicated tickets, the median gains 0.00 pp, P90 gains 3.45 pp, and only 3 gain 10 pp or more.

A bigger problem is hiding here. 36 CSPs have ~100% of their tickets closed by the CC agent — they never close in the app at all; 67 are above 25%, against a median of 4.8%. For them the sync fix is not a rescue: the worst case (203 tickets) goes from 2.5% to 13.8% within-TAT and is still failing. That is an app-adoption problem wearing this problem's clothes, and it needs its own look.

Q4What is the uptick? Does CSP CSAT improve?

CSAT cannot be estimated today — and that is a finding, not a hedge. The customer-rating pipeline (539k historical ticket ratings, mean 4.55) died on 7 Jul 2026, and the actor id needed to classify a closure only exists from mid-Jul 2026. The two windows do not overlap, so 0 of 42,626 cohort tickets carry a rating. Repairing that connector is a prerequisite for any CSAT claim — here or anywhere else in CX.

What is measurable is the waste table in §03. And one piece of counter-evidence that makes the case stronger:

Closure classTicketsReopenedReopen rate
A · CSP closed it36,0344,53612.59%
C · Kapture first, CSP later3,4673349.63%
B · Kapture only2,059522.53%

Tickets the CC agent closed reopen less than tickets the CSP closed. The agent closes after confirming with the customer; the CSP closes on their own assertion, which no validation can currently block (F-01). So propagating a Kapture closure is not trading accuracy for speed — on the only outcome measure we have, it is propagating the more reliable of the two signals.

07

What this measurement cannot tell you

  • Nothing before mid-July 2026. The closing actor is identified by two sentinel ids (1234567890 = CSP/SRS path, 137439087976 = Kapture webhook) that the .NET→Java rewrite introduced. July is half-instrumented; August is the first trustworthy month.
  • Internet Issues only. Shifting and installation-defect classes are unmeasured, and shifting emits no Quality signal at all by design (F-05).
  • No CSAT, no ticket comments. Both DynamoDB connectors stopped on 7 Jul 2026.
  • Reopens. 9.7% of tickets carry more than one complaint; the first complaint's SLA is used. Splitting single from multi does not change the conclusion.
  • Kapture's resolver-identity columns are broken and were not used: CLOSED_BY is 100% NULL, FIRST_RESOLVED_ROLE is the literal string 'rohit' on every row, and FIRST_RESOLVED_BY_ACCOUNT_ID always equals the assigned partner. The whole measurement had to be rebuilt from the event log.

Recommendation. Build the Kapture→SRS closure webhook. Justify it on 1,078 wasted technician dispatches and 1,774 zombie complaints a month, on a share growing ~50% month-on-month, and on 1,770 Quality signals that never fire. Carry the 410 unfair breaches as a fairness sweetener, not as the headline — it is one point.

Spin out separately: the 36 CSPs who never close in the app, and the dead rating and comment connectors, which block CSAT measurement well beyond this problem.
